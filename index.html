how to play
1
Connect Phantom. This is only your payout address — winnings are sent straight here. The game never holds your wallet's funds.
2
Fund the burner. A throwaway key generated in your browser. It signs every click for you, so there is no wallet popup in the middle of a race. Only put in what you are willing to click away.
3
Click the fuse. You become the holder and the fuse resets. If it burns all the way down while you are still the holder, the pot is yours.

Nobody runs the payout. When the fuse dies, the next click settles the previous round automatically and pays the winner — or anyone can crank it manually and earn 1% for doing so. There is no operator, no admin key over the pot, and no queue to wait in.
how this pot is funded
Every click pays into this pot directly — 100% of the click price, no house cut. On top of that, $FUSE trading fees feed the pot too: a fixed share of every pump.fun creator fee is swept in automatically, on top of whatever players are putting in by clicking.
