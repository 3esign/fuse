nobody runs the payout
When the fuse dies, the next click settles the previous round automatically and pays the winner — or anyone at all can crank it manually and earn 1% for doing so. There is no server, no operator, no admin key over the pot and no queue to wait in. The program has exactly three instructions — init, click, settle — and no withdraw, no pause and no fee setter. The pot is a program-derived address with no private key in existence.
Reading the upgrade authority from the chain…
The burner is a throwaway Ed25519 keypair generated in your browser (WebCrypto) and kept in this browser's local storage. It never touches Phantom and never leaves this device, so your loss is bounded by its balance. Winnings always go to your beneficiary (Phantom) address, never to the burner.
how this pot is funded
Every click pays into the pot directly — 100% of the click price, no house cut. On top of that, $FUSE trading fees feed the pot: a fixed share of every pump.fun creator fee is swept in automatically. You never need to hold $FUSE to play.
